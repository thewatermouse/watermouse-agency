Performance Max · eCommerce

Performance Max, run by someone who can see inside it.

PMax will spend whatever you give it. Whether that spend builds profit or just looks busy depends entirely on how it's set up.

Performance Max is a black box on purpose — one campaign type across Search, Shopping, YouTube, Display, Gmail and Maps, with Google's automation deciding where the money goes. Handed to it without structure, it quietly leans on your cheapest, easiest conversions — usually brand and remarketing — and reports a flattering ROAS while doing little you couldn't have done for less.

Done properly, PMax is one of the most powerful tools in eCommerce paid media. The difference is entirely in the setup: a clean product feed, deliberate audience signals, brand traffic separated out, and asset groups built around real product economics — not one bucket for the whole catalogue.

The short version

We run PMax with the feed, the signals and the exclusions that make it accountable — and we report on incremental profit, not the number PMax wants to show you.

Included in every plan alongside Search and Shopping — PMax is a component of the account, managed as one system, never a standalone spend.
What it takes

Getting PMax to actually work.

01
A feed that carries the campaign

In PMax the product feed is the campaign. Titles, product types, GTINs, images and custom labels decide what Google can match and show. We optimise the feed first, because no signal or budget fixes a weak one.

02
Audience signals, not guesses

Signals steer PMax toward the right people faster. We build them from your real converters, high-value segments and search themes — not the default 'let it learn' that burns budget while it figures out who your customer is.

03
Brand and non-brand, separated

Left alone, PMax eats brand searches you'd win anyway and books the credit. We use brand exclusions and account structure so PMax is measured on the new demand it actually creates.

04
Asset groups by product economics

A €40 consumable and a €400 hero product should not sit in the same bucket at the same target. We split asset groups by margin and role so budget follows profit, not volume.

FAQ

Performance Max, answered.

Is Performance Max good for eCommerce?

Yes — when it's set up correctly. PMax is well suited to eCommerce because it combines Shopping, Search and other inventory around your product feed. But it rewards good structure and punishes lazy setup: a strong feed, clear audience signals and brand separation are what separate a profitable PMax account from one that just spends.

Why is my PMax spending everything on brand terms?

Because PMax defaults to the cheapest conversions it can find, and your own brand searches are the cheapest of all. Without brand exclusions or account structure to separate them, PMax will happily claim traffic you'd have won for free and report it as performance. We fix this with brand exclusions and structure so you can see the incremental result.

Performance Max vs Standard Shopping — which should I use?

It depends on control and data. PMax typically reaches more inventory and can outperform on scale, but it hides placement and search-term detail. Standard Shopping gives you more transparency and control. For many eCommerce accounts the right answer is a deliberate combination, structured so they complement rather than compete — which is exactly what we set up.

How long until PMax stabilises after changes?

Plan on roughly 2–4 weeks for the learning phase to settle after a significant change, and longer for full seasonal signals. This is one reason we work on a 3-month minimum — meaningful PMax optimisation compounds over weeks, not days.
