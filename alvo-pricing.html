Common questions

Everything you want to know.

Why a 3-month minimum? Is that a lock-in?
The 3-month minimum exists for one reason: that's how long it takes to build, test, and let optimisation compound. Month one is audit and rebuild, month two is testing, month three is when results start stacking. Asking for results in week four is like planting a seed and digging it up two weeks later. After that, cancel any time with 30 days' notice.
What do you need from us to get started?
Access to your Google Ads account, Google Analytics 4, and Google Merchant Center (for Shopping). A 60-minute onboarding call to align on goals, targets, margins, and messaging. That's it — we take it from there.
Do you handle ad creative and copy?
Ad copy is included in every plan — we write, test, and iterate on it. For display and YouTube, we provide creative direction and briefs. Final asset production (video, imagery) sits with your creative team or a partner, but we'll tell you exactly what to make and why.
We're already working with an agency. Can you take over?
Yes — and this is actually common. We do a full audit of the existing account first, identify what to keep and what to fix, and manage the transition carefully to avoid performance gaps. We've done this many times and know how to do it cleanly.
What eCommerce platforms do you work with?
Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento, and most other major platforms. As long as you have a product feed and a Google Merchant Center account, we can work with you. If your setup is more custom, we'll assess it on the audit call.
Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?
Yes — at the end of any monthly cycle. We'll also proactively recommend a plan change if we think it makes sense for your growth stage. We'd rather have you on the right plan than the most expensive one.
