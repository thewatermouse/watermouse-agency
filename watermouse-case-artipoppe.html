Scaling a premium brand without diluting what makes it premium.

Artipoppe sells high-end baby carriers to design-conscious parents across Europe. Growing paid revenue meant finding more of the right customers — not just more customers.

Context

A luxury product with a deliberately narrow audience.

Artipoppe is a Dutch premium baby carrier brand known for its design-led aesthetic and high-quality materials. Their products sit at the top end of the market — customers are deliberate, research-driven buyers who care as much about brand identity as product quality.

This isn't a brand you scale by casting a wide net. Reaching the wrong audience wastes budget and risks positioning damage. Every euro of paid spend needed to reach buyers who were genuinely ready to invest in a premium product.

The challenge

Growth that couldn't come at the cost of the brand.

Premium brands live and die by perception. Paid needed to grow revenue without leaning on the discounting and broad-reach tactics that quietly erode a luxury position — and without pouring budget into audiences that would never convert at a premium price point.

The problem

Paid coverage was reaching too broad an audience — spending against low-intent traffic and treating every product the same, regardless of margin or genuine demand.

The solution

A rebuild of the Shopping and Search structure around the best-margin, highest-intent products — tighter targeting, cleaner feed segmentation, and bidding aligned to profitable conversions rather than raw volume.

How we did it

Precision over reach.

01
Audit & feed cleanup

Reviewed account structure, search terms, and the product feed — cutting wasted spend and fixing the data Google actually bids on.

02
Rebuild around intent & margin

Restructured Shopping and Search to prioritise the products and queries that attract genuine premium buyers — not bargain hunters.

03
Optimise for profitable growth

Aligned bidding to profitable ROAS, tested audiences and creative, and scaled only what held margin as spend grew.

Results

More of the right customers — not just more customers.

Paid became a reliable channel for reaching high-intent premium buyers, with a structure built to scale without diluting the brand. Specific performance figures available on request.
