Google Ads · Netherlands

Google Ads for Dutch eCommerce, run by someone who knows the market.

The Netherlands is one of the most efficient eCommerce markets in Europe — and one of the easiest to run generically and leave money on the table.

Dutch consumers are online, comfortable buying, and price-aware. CPCs are relatively efficient, English converts alongside Dutch, and iDEAL is the default at checkout. That maturity is an advantage — but only if your Google Ads reflect how the market actually behaves, from bilingual search intent to the Sinterklaas peak that front-loads the Q4 gifting season before Christmas even arrives.

Most of Watermouse's hands-on European work has been with Dutch brands — TicketSwap, Artipoppe and LandLife are all NL-based. That's real, specific experience with how Dutch eCommerce searches, converts and competes, not a generic playbook with a flag on it.

What running Google Ads in the NL actually takes.

01
Bilingual search intent

Dutch and English queries both convert in the Netherlands, often for the same product. We structure campaigns and copy for both, instead of forcing one language and missing half the demand.

Seasonality that isn't the UK's

FAQ

Google Ads in the Netherlands, answered.

Do you work with eCommerce brands based in the Netherlands?

Yes — Dutch eCommerce is where most of our hands-on European work has been. TicketSwap, Artipoppe and LandLife are all NL-based brands whose Google Ads were run directly by the person you'd work with. We understand how the Dutch market searches, converts and competes.

Should Dutch campaigns be in Dutch or English?

Usually both. Dutch consumers frequently search in English as well as Dutch, sometimes for the same product, so forcing a single language leaves demand on the table. We structure campaigns and ad copy to capture both, weighted to what actually converts for your category.

Do I need a local agency to run Google Ads in the Netherlands?

No — you need someone who understands the NL market, which is not the same as being physically in it. Watermouse operates on European hours with real Dutch-market experience, and the lean setup means you get senior, hands-on work at a fraction of a traditional local agency retainer.

How is Dutch eCommerce seasonality different?

The big one is Sinterklaas in early December, which pulls a large share of gifting demand forward of Christmas — on top of Black Friday. Planning budgets and bids around the true NL calendar, rather than a UK/US template, is one of the clearest ways to out-execute competitors.
